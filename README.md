# 🚗 CampusRide — Safe, Fair & Verified Commutes for Day Scholars

> **Built by a 1st-Semester Day Scholar** | Solving real campus evening transport problems through smart frontend design.

---

## 📌 Problem & Personal Motivation

As a **day scholar**, I personally experienced a major hassle every time late campus events, quiz, sessionals, or society meetings ended:

1. **Safety Concerns:** Finding safe transport options late at night while traveling alone is stressful.
2. **Heavy Overcharging:** Local drivers often exploit stranded students by charging **₹300+** for short distances that usually cost just **₹50**.

as a first - semester student, my focus was in identifying the real problem, designing the user flow , and structuring the solution . i used AI tools to build this prototype which helped me understand how javascripts handles the logic and how css brings the design.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

* 🔍 **Smart Ride Search:** Search available pools by typing your destination neighborhood (e.g., *Model Town*, *Urban Estate*), departure date, and time.
* 🛡️ **Campus-Authorized Transport:** Verified vehicle registration badges (`PB-11-XX-XXXX`) and clear campus pickup points (e.g., *Campus Gate 2 Plaza*).
* ⚖️ **Fixed Student Rates:** Standardized fares (₹40–₹70) — zero surge pricing or late-night bargaining.
* 🎟️ **Digital Pass & Booking Confirmation:** Instant pass generation with a unique booking code (`CR-XXXX`) to show drivers at campus gates.
* 📂 **"My Bookings" Tracker:** A dedicated tab for students to view, track, and manage active reserved rides.
* 🚘 **Peer Ride Sharing ("Offer a Ride"):** Enables senior day scholars with bikes or cars to list empty seats and share fuel costs.
* 📱 **Fully Responsive UI:** Smooth layout designed for both smartphones and desktops.
